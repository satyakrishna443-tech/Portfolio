About

A little about me

As a Sr Fraud Specialist at Affirm, I apply my expertise in fraud prevention, data analysis, and business insights to protect the company and its customers from fraud and financial crimes. I have over 15 years of experience in fraud operations, risk management, and anti-counterfeiting across various industries and markets.

In my current role, I work with the international risk operations team, overseeing fraud detection, investigation, and resolution for Affirm's global expansion. I also partner with cross-functional stakeholders to design and implement fraud policies, processes, and systems that align with the company's goals and values. I am passionate about using data and strategy to combat fraud and deliver a safe and transparent experience for users.
